MIS Reporting Assignment

  1. Prepare monthly sales and purchase summaries.
  2. Identify top 5 customers by sales value.
  3. Identify top 5 suppliers by purchase value.
  4. Find the top 10 selling stock items by quantity and value.
  5. Compare cash sales with credit sales.
  6. Prepare customer outstanding with ageing.
  7. Prepare supplier outstanding with ageing.
  8. Analyse overdue receivables and payables.
  9. Compare gross profit by month.
  10. Compare gross profit by stock group/item.
  11. Review closing stock by godown.
  12. Identify slow-moving and fast-moving items.
  13. Check negative stock and unusual stock balances.
  14. Analyse purchase returns and sales returns.
  15. Review discount allowed versus discount received.
  16. Review freight inward/outward and their impact on margins.
  17. Prepare cash and bank position.
  18. Review bank reconciliation items.
  19. Prepare Profit & Loss and Balance Sheet.
  20. Review Trial Balance and ledger balances.
  21. Perform ratio analysis.
  22. Review GST sales, purchases, input tax credit and output tax.
  23. Prepare GST rate-wise taxable value and tax summary.
  24. Review party-wise sales and purchase trends.
  25. Compare monthly revenue, expenses and net profit.
  26. Identify customers with declining purchases.
  27. Identify suppliers with high purchase concentration.
  28. Analyse inventory carrying value.
  29. Prepare management commentary with 5 findings and 5 action points.
  30. Export selected MIS reports to Excel/PDF for management review.
